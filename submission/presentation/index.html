CRYPTOGRAPHIC ASSUMPTIONS

The secret is a scalar, not a preimage.

Adaptor signatures buy scriptless, unlinkable swaps — and move atomicity onto discrete log.

PRIMITIVEASSUMPTIONUNDER A CRQC

Strictly weaker than the HTLC it replaces

An HTLC's secret is a hash preimage: Grover halves it, 256 bits become 128, and the atomicity survives. Our secret is a scalar with T = t·G published in the agreement. Shor recovers t and claims both legs.

Even if both chains adopted PQ signatures, a secp256k1 adaptor swap would still leak its witness.

BTC leg · adaptordiscrete log
ZEC leg · BIP-199 hashlockhash — already PQ-shaped

Migration path is the construction we already ship, not new cryptography.

Inherited, not chosen: Bitcoin consensus has no post-quantum signature scheme, so every BTC swap today is Schnorr-based; P2TR additionally exposes the output key at rest. Lattice (LAS) and isogeny (IAS) adaptor signatures exist in research but need a soft fork. Exposure is real-time, not harvest-now — a settled swap cannot be stolen retroactively.
Not a claimed property · tracked as a design assumption, not a mitigation
